## Independent UX verdict (Claude, 2026-11-17)

Method: emil-design-eng and break-ui read first; 30 PNGs opened (form empty/filled/errors/picker/rate-limited, edit-stale, draft list/menu/card, cancelled card, cancel empty/filled/max-reason/pending/stale/long-title, delete draft/cancelled; both themes, desktop 1440 and mobile 390, three roles); source read for toast offset (`components/ui/sonner-runtime.tsx`, `lib/use-sticky-bar.ts`) and capture helper (`tests/e2e/screens.ts`). 320 px and 200% zoom were not reviewable: no captures exist.

**Verdict: PASS WITH ISSUES.** Visual quality of what exists is good: clear hierarchy, consistent Ukrainian copy, dark-mode contrast holds (error red, danger button, banners), dialogs explain consequences (cancel vs delete), safe action on the left and danger on the right, 200-char title and 500-char reason stay inside the modal and sheet, no horizontal overflow. S3.6 cannot close: the §10 matrix is only partly captured and one mobile toast defect needs re-evidence.
